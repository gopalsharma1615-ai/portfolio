# Gopal Sharma: education, skills and achievements

## Education
Gopal Sharma holds a Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics from the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Calicut
(2017-2019, CGPA 8.74/10). His M.Sc. project was "Renormalization of Quantum Field Theory", covering quantum
electrodynamics (QED), Feynman diagrams, scattering processes, the Ward-Takahashi identity, dimensional
regularization and one-loop renormalization. He also holds a Diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Machine
Learning from the University of Hyderabad (2021-2022) and a B.Sc. in Physical Science from S.G.T.B. Khalsa
College, University of Delhi (2014-2017, 82.3%).

## Achievements
Gopal secured All India Rank 81 in CSIR-UGC NET (Physical Sciences) in 2019 and All India Rank 469 in IIT JAM
(Physics) in 2017.

## Physics background applied to AI
Gopal uses his physics training in AI work. His qft-renorm-ai project turns the M.Sc. renormalization work into
verified SymPy code and uses sparse regression to rediscover the QCD beta-function coefficient beta0 = 11 -
2nf/3 from running-coupling data. His graph retrieval research treats multi-hop question answering as a
reachability problem on a graph, much as a physicist treats paths and propagation.
